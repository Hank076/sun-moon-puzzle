Sun & Moon Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Sun & Moon (“the app”) is provided by the developer listed on its App Store page. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app itself collects

The app does not collect personal information and does not require an account. Your game records, such as best times, daily puzzle results, history, and puzzles in progress, are stored only on your device. You can delete your history in the app, and deleting the app removes all of this data.

Purchases

The “Remove All Ads” purchase is processed by Apple. The app receives only confirmation that the purchase was made and never sees your payment information. See Apple’s privacy policy for details.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, the SDK may collect:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information
  • Performance data, such as app launch time, hang rate, or energy usage
  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) or other app- or developer-scoped identifiers
  • Advertising data, such as the ads you have seen
  • Product interactions, such as taps and ad views

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: The advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, the app still shows ads, but they are not personalized using the advertising identifier.
  • Ad consent: In regions where it is required, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before using data for ads. You can review or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings › Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Remove ads: After you buy “Remove All Ads”, the app stops requesting ads and no longer starts the Google Mobile Ads SDK when it opens.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@lab076.dev. More help is on the Support page.

Sun & Moon 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

Sun & Moon App（以下稱「本 App」）由 App Store 頁面上列出的開發者提供。本政策說明本 App 使用哪些資訊，以及使用的原因。

本 App 收集的資訊

本 App 不收集個人資料，也不需要帳號。你的遊戲紀錄，包含最佳紀錄、每日一題成績、解題紀錄與進行中的題目，只存在你的裝置上。你可以在 App 內刪除解題紀錄；刪除 App 會清除所有這些資料。

購買

「移除所有廣告」的購買由 Apple 處理。本 App 只會收到購買完成的確認，不會取得你的付款資訊。詳情請參考 Apple 的隱私權政策。

廣告

本 App 透過 Google Mobile Ads SDK 顯示 Google AdMob 提供的廣告。依據 Google 的說明，這個 SDK 可能收集：

  • IP 位址，可能用來推估裝置的大略位置
  • 當機紀錄與診斷資訊
  • 效能資料，例如 App 啟動時間、卡頓頻率或耗電量
  • 裝置識別碼，例如廣告識別碼（IDFA），或限定於 App 或開發者範圍的識別碼
  • 廣告資料，例如你看過的廣告
  • 互動資料，例如點擊與廣告觀看

Google 使用這些資訊來顯示與評估廣告、防止詐騙，以及改善服務。詳情請參考 Google 隱私權政策與Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

你的選擇

  • 追蹤：只有在本 App 詢問時你選擇允許追蹤，才會使用廣告識別碼。你可以隨時到 iOS「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 追蹤」修改。不允許追蹤時，本 App 仍會顯示廣告，但不會用廣告識別碼提供個人化廣告。
  • 廣告同意：在法規要求的地區，例如歐洲經濟區與英國，本 App 會在將資料用於廣告之前徵求你的同意。你可以隨時在 App 的「設定 › 廣告隱私權選項」查看或撤回同意。
  • 移除廣告：購買「移除所有廣告」後，本 App 不再請求廣告，開啟時也不再啟動 Google Mobile Ads SDK。

兒童

本 App 不是針對 13 歲以下兒童設計。

政策變更

本政策如有變更，會在本頁公布更新後的版本與新的生效日期。

聯絡我們
